Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last Updated / Son Güncelleme: November 22, 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy is important to us. Wobbly Bottle: Party Game ("App"), developed by Mert Koçbıyıkoğlu, does not require mandatory account registration and does not collect or sell personally identifiable information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect personal data such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.

  • Lobby & Multiplayer Sessions: Temporary game room state (active room code, player avatars, turn index) is synchronized in real time via Firebase Realtime Database. Rooms and temporary session states are automatically deleted when the game session ends.
  • Local Device Storage: Sound preferences, selected language, and unlocked cosmetic themes are stored strictly locally on your device using local key-value storage.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates standard industry services to operate:

  • Google AdMob: Serves standard mobile advertisements to support free gameplay. AdMob may process anonymous device advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Used exclusively for real-time multiplayer bottle rotation and question synchronization.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Security

All network communications between the App and synchronized servers are encrypted in transit via industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS/WSS).
