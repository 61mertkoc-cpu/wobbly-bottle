## 🚀 Key Features

- 📲 **Play on Your Own Phones:** Everyone joins the room using a private 6-digit code or QR code (similar to Jackbox or Kahoot).
- 🌀 **Real-Time Physics Bottle:** The bottle wobbles, bends, and tilts dynamically as it spins, synchronizing smoothly across every device with zero latency.
- 🎮 **3 Exciting Game Modes:**
  1. **Table Mode (Single Phone):** Classic pass-and-play in the center of the table.
  2. **House Party (In-Person):** Everyone in the living room on their own phone—close-contact dares, couples chemistry, and drinking penalties.
  3. **Remote Party (Online):** Long-distance play across different cities over Discord, Zoom, or FaceTime with screenshot dares, photo proofs, and voice confessions.
- 🐔 **5 Hilarious Spinning Objects:**
  - Classic Soda Bottle
  - Squeaky Rubber Chicken (with sound effects!)
  - Wobbly Green Pickle
  - Flying Slipper
  - VIP Golden Champagne Bottle
- 🃏 **1,000+ Handcrafted Cards Across 5 Categories:**
  - Ice Breaker
  - Deep Secrets
  - Extreme Dares & Shots
  - Flirt & Couples
  - Spicy +18 (VIP)
