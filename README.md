## ⚡ Key Features

- 📱 **Play on Your Own Phones:** Everyone joins the room using a private 6-digit PIN (similar to Jackbox or Kahoot).
- 🔄 **Real-Time Physics Bottle:** The bottle wobbles, bends, and tilts dynamically as it spins, synchronizing smoothly across every device at 60 FPS.
- 🎯 **3 Exciting Game Modes:**
  1. **Table Mode (Single Phone):** Classic pass-and-play in the center of the table (zero Wi-Fi required, perfect for dates and camping).
  2. **House Party (In-Person):** Everyone in the living room on their own phone with drinking penalties and room sync.
  3. **Remote Party (Online):** Long-distance play across different cities over Discord, Zoom, or FaceTime with voice confessions and screenshot dares.
- 🐔 **5 Hilarious Spinning Objects:**
  - Classic Glass Soda Bottle
  - Squeaky Rubber Chicken (with authentic squawk sound!)
  - Wobbly Green Pickle
  - Flying Slipper
  - VIP Golden Champagne Bottle
- 🃏 **1,000+ Handcrafted Cards Across 5 Languages:**
  - English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French.
