## 🌟 Key Features

- 📱 **Play on Your Own Phones:** Everyone joins the room using a private 6-digit code or QR code (similar to Jackbox or Kahoot).
- 🍾 **Real-Time Physics Bottle:** The bottle wobbles, bends, and tilts dynamically as it spins, synchronizing smoothly across every device.
- 🌐 **3 Exciting Game Modes:**
  1. **Table Mode (Single Phone):** Classic pass-and-play in the center of the table.
  2. **House Party (In-Person):** Everyone in the living room on their own phone—close-contact dares, couples chemistry, and party challenges.
  3. **Remote Party (Online):** Long-distance play across different cities over Discord, Zoom, or FaceTime with screenshot dares, photo proofs, and voice confessions.
- 🐔 **5 Hilarious Spinning Objects:**
  - Classic Soda Bottle
  - Squeaky Rubber Chicken
  - Wobbly Green Pickle
  - Flying Slipper
  - VIP Golden Champagne Bottle
- 🎯 **1,000+ Handcrafted Cards Across 5 Categories:**
  - **Ice Breaker:** Warm up the vibe with funny, lighthearted prompts.
  - **Deep Secrets:** Unfiltered confessions you have never told anyone.
  - **Extreme Dares:** Do the dare or take a penalty shot!
  - **Flirt & Couples:** Romantic chemistry and bold questions.
  - **Spicy +18 (VIP):** Uncensored adult party fun.
  - **Custom Cards:** Write your own inside jokes and personal dares.
