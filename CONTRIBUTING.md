# Contributing to Wobbly Bottle

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Wobbly Bottle: Party Game**!

## How You Can Contribute
1. **Suggest New Funny Objects:** We are always looking for ridiculous spinning physics objects (rubber chickens, slippers, pickles, frying pans, etc.). Open an issue or discussion!
2. **Card Prompts & Translations:** Suggest funny, deep, or extreme Truth or Dare prompts in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, or French.
3. **Bug Reports & Improvements:** If you discover a desynchronization bug or visual glitch, submit an Issue with your device model and OS version.
