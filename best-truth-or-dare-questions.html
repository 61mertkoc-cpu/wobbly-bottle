🧊 Ice Breaker & Hilarious Truth Questions

Lighthearted and funny questions to get everyone laughing and comfortable.

  1. What is the most embarrassing lie you have told to get out of social plans?
  2. What is your ultimate guilty pleasure TV show or guilty music track?
  3. If you could swap lives with anyone in this room for 24 hours, who would it be?
  4. What is the weirdest thing in your recent Google search history?
  5. What is the most ridiculous fashion phase you went through?
  6. If stranded on a deserted island, who in this room would you eat first to survive?
  7. What childish fear do you secretly still have?
  8. What is the worst date you have ever been on in your entire life?
  9. Have you ever practiced kissing on your pillow or hand?
  10. Who in this room is most likely to end up on a reality TV show?

🔥 Spicy & Deep Confessions

High-voltage questions that dig into crushes, past secrets, and relationship drama.

  1. Have you ever had a crush on someone in this room right now?
  2. When was the last time you stalked your ex on social media?
  3. What is the biggest romantic red flag you ignored because they were attractive?
  4. Have you ever sent a text message you immediately regretted within 2 seconds?
  5. What is a secret you swore you would take to the grave?
  6. Have you ever had feelings for a close friend's significant other?
  7. What is the longest you have ever gone without showering?
  8. Have you ever ghosted someone who did not deserve it?
  9. What is the wildest thing on your personal bucket list?
  10. Who in this room would you choose to take to a romantic getaway?

⚡ Extreme Dares & Party Penalties

Bold challenges to get people moving, laughing, or taking drinks!

  1. Let the group craft a 2-word text and send it to the 4th person in your recent contacts.
  2. Do your best impression of a squeaky rubber chicken until someone laughs.
  3. Show the last 3 photos in your camera roll to the entire circle.
  4. Call a random pizza shop on speaker and ask if they deliver burritos.
  5. Let the player to your right restyle your hair however they want for the rest of the game.
  6. Sniff the shoe of the person across from you and give it a rating out of 10.
  7. Stare into the eyes of the person to your left for 30 seconds without blinking or smiling.
  8. Speak in a dramatic Shakespearean accent for the next 3 rounds.
  9. Do 15 pushups or take a penalty drink right now.
  10. Let the group look through your Spotify Recently Played history without judgment.
