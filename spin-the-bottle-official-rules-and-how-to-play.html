1. Standard Rules & Setup

The fundamental mechanics of an honest Spin the Bottle game.

  1. Players sit in a circle. In modern play, one phone is placed in Table Mode at the center, or players connect their own phones via room code.
  2. The spinner acts as the inquisitor; the person pointed to must face the challenge.
  3. Choice must be declared before the bottle stops: TRUTH or DARE.
  4. The Safety Rule: Anyone can pass on a question by taking a predetermined penalty or using a one-time free pass.

2. Game Variations

Tailor the rules to match your party vibe.

  1. Classic Truth or Dare: Deep personal secrets and hilarious physical dares.
  2. Pre-Drinks & Penalty Shots: Failure or refusal to complete a prompt results in a drink penalty.
  3. Date Night & Icebreakers: Gentle, funny questions designed to eliminate awkward silences without crossing boundaries.
  4. Remote FaceTime Play: Synchronized across long distance with photo proof and voice recording challenges.

3. The Multi-Device Evolution

Why the single-phone squeeze is a thing of the past.

  1. Physical glass bottles scratch hardwood floors and lead to endless arguments about who the neck pointed to.
  2. Classic party apps force 12 people to crowd around a 6-inch phone display.
  3. Wobbly Bottle lets up to 15 smartphones connect to one room, rendering identical 60 FPS physics on every screen.

Sıkça Sorulan Sorular

What is the minimum number of players for Spin the Bottle?

You can play with as few as 2 players (perfect for couples using Table Mode) up to 15+ players at college parties.

What happens if a player refuses a dare?

In standard party rules, refusing a dare requires taking a penalty shot or performing an alternative consequence agreed by the room.

Can everyone play on their own phone without passing one device?

Yes! Wobbly Bottle allows up to 15 players to join a private room via a 6-digit PIN and watch the synchronized bottle spin on their own screens.
