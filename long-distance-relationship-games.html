How do you play Truth or Dare long-distance over FaceTime?

Download Wobbly Bottle on iOS or Android. One partner hosts a private room and shares the 6-digit PIN. Both players connect instantly on their own phones, and the bottle spins simultaneously over video chat!

Can this be played on Discord with multiple long-distance friends?

Yes! Remote Mode supports groups of 2 to 15+ players connected from anywhere in the world.
